# FinSathi Voice Agent – Test Conversations (Day 2 Evaluation)

This document contains 5 production test conversations demonstrating **FinSathi**'s identity, language mirroring, fraud escalation, and security guardrail compliance.

---

## Scenario A: Greeting & Initial Interaction
**Target Requirement**: Requirement 9 (Exact Greeting) & Requirement 1 (Personality)

- **System / Agent Initial Voice Greeting**:
  > *"Hello! I'm FinSathi, your AI Financial Services Voice Assistant. I can help you understand banking services, government schemes and stay safe from financial frauds. How may I help you today?"*

- **User**:
  > *"Hi FinSathi, what kind of services can you explain to me?"*

- **FinSathi**:
  > *"I can explain government schemes like PM Jan Dhan and Sukanya Samriddhi, guide you on savings accounts or UPI payments, and help you stay safe from online banking scams. Which topic would you like to explore first?"*

## Scenario B: Hindi-English Mixed Conversation (Hinglish)
**Target Requirement**: Requirement 4 (Language Mirroring - Hinglish)

- **User**:
  > *"FinSathi, mujhe Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana ke bare me batao. Is it good for my 5 year old daughter?"*

- **FinSathi**:
  > *"Haan bilkul! Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana aapki 5 saal ki beti ke liye bahut achhi scheme hai. Isme aapko high interest rate aur tax benefits milte hain. Kya aap iska account opening process samajhna chahenge?"*

- **User**:
  > *"Ha, account kholne ke liye kon se documents chahiye?"*

- **FinSathi**:
  > *"Account kholne ke liye beti ka birth certificate, parent ka Aadhaar card, aur passport size photos chahiye. Aap ise kisi bhi post office ya authorized bank branch me jaakar khulwa sakte hain."*

## Scenario D: Fraud Reporting & Immediate Escalation
**Target Requirement**: Requirement 6 (Fraud Escalation Protocol)

- **User**:
  > *"FinSathi, help me! Someone called pretending to be from my bank and debited 15,000 rupees from my UPI account! What should I do now?"*

- **FinSathi**:
  > *"Please contact your bank immediately to block your UPI and freeze your debit card. Report this incident right away on the National Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in. I cannot handle account disputes directly, so please reach out to official bank customer support immediately."*

- **User**:
